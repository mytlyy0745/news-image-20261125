A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026. The lead story is the Brooklyn jury convicting Huawei on 10 of 12 counts — bank and wire fraud, sanctions violations and stealing US trade secrets — with six more stories below: Ukraine sinking the Russian corvette Merkury off Libya, a Houthi drone hitting Riyadh's PIF Tower, Trump awarding Rudy Giuliani the Medal of Freedom, a breach at the CIA and the Director of National Intelligence's office, NATO vowing space defence over reports of a Russian nuclear-armed satellite, and England crushing Australia by eight wickets in the second T20I. A footer strip adds four more items from the day. `draw.py` renders the page with Pillow into a 1024×1024 PNG; `main.py` (standard library only) validates the PNG and copies it byte-identical to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
